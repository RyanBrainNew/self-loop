# Decisions

> Settled decisions that later agents must not silently overturn. When a
> decision changes, add a new entry and mark the old one as superseded.

## Format

### YYYY-MM-DD - Short title

- Decision: What was chosen.
- Why: Constraints, trade-offs, and evidence.
- Consequences: What becomes easier or harder.
- Supersedes: Link to an earlier entry, or "none".
